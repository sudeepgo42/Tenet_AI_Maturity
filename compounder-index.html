The AI Readiness Diagnostic

A Tenet Advisory & Investments diagnostic

Buying AI tools is the easy part. Turning them into real momentum, where your strategy, safeguards, and everyday habits reinforce one another, is where the hard work begins.

This diagnostic is designed to show you where that momentum is building, and where things might be getting stuck.

Think of this as a mirror, not an exam. No individual person's score will be reviewed or reported on. There are no right grades or bonus points for picking the option that sounds best on paper; the only useful answer is the honest one. Across the questions, you'll simply choose the scenario that best reflects what daily life looks like across your teams today, so we can plan the appropriate tools, support and processes for the team.

If you aren't sure about any answer, choosing "I don't know" is completely fine. In fact, uncovering those blind spots is half the point. Take your time, be candid, and share your perspective.

About the methodology

Every dimension is scored from three or four questions, each with four answers describing increasing maturity. Every question also offers "I don't know", which is left out of the score rather than counted as the worst answer; a dimension needs real answers to at least half its questions before it is scored at all. Scores are normalised to a scale of 0 to 100 and mapped to four levels: Emerging, Adopter, Integrator, Leader. Where both tiers are completed, the combined score weights organisational readiness at 60 per cent and individual proficiency at 40 per cent, because individual skill can only compound where the organisation has built the foundation for it to land.
