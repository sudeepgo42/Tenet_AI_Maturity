The Compounder Index

Tenet's AI Readiness Diagnostic

Most organisations have bought AI tools. Few have built the strategy, governance, and capability to get value from them. This diagnostic measures both: how ready your organisation is, and how well your people actually use what you have given them.

Answers are scored against behaviour, not opinion. Each question describes four real states of practice, from absent to advanced, and you pick the one closest to your reality. There is no reward for picking the impressive-sounding answer, only for picking the true one.

About the methodology

Every dimension is scored from four questions, each with four answers describing increasing maturity. Scores are normalised to a scale of 0 to 100 and mapped to four levels: Bystander, Adopter, Integrator, Compounder. Where both tiers are completed, the combined score weights organisational readiness at 60 per cent and individual proficiency at 40 per cent, because individual skill can only compound where the organisation has built the foundation for it to land.
